SpareX · In Development

Automotive spare-parts commerce, connected to the vehicle.

SpareX is being developed to support retail customers and automotive trade businesses through vehicle-based discovery, ordering and procurement.

Platform under development · Not yet publicly launched
Retail · B2C

For motorcycle owners

  • Planned vehicle-based parts search
  • Product details and availability
  • Cart, checkout and order tracking
  • Customer purchase history
Trade · B2B

For workshops and dealers

  • Business account verification
  • Business-specific commercial pricing
  • Bulk ordering and procurement
  • Order management and invoicing

These are intended product capabilities. Availability, pricing, payments and integrations are subject to development and testing.

Product Vision

Parts and service in one ecosystem.

SpareX is intended to connect parts discovery with service job requirements, reducing manual coordination for garages and customers.

Planned integration areas

  • Shared vehicle and product catalogue
  • Business and retail pricing rules
  • Inventory visibility and order lifecycle
  • Garage service parts requirements
